# ADR 0001: Part data in the repository and in a local cache

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-22

## Context

Every part in the BOMs needs data for design and simulation: datasheets, PCB footprints, symbols, 3D models, and electrical, electromagnetic, thermal and mechanical simulation models. Much of what manufacturers publish cannot be redistributed, and OpenDAP's libraries are public.

## Decision

- **In the repository** (OpenDAP's own work, under this repository's licences):
  - symbols and footprints drawn from the datasheets (in `kicad-lib`), following KiCad's library conventions;
  - 3D models generated by OpenDAP from the datasheet dimensions (scripted, so they are reproducible; in `kicad-lib`);
  - one spec sheet per part, `<manufacturer>/<MPN>.md` in this repository, transcribing the data the project needs;
  - the manifest `manifest.toml` in this repository, listing every external file with its official URL, revision, date and sha256.
- **In a local cache** (`~/.cache/opendap/parts/`, never committed) unless the file's licence explicitly allows redistribution: datasheets, manufacturer STEP models, IBIS and SPICE models, S-parameter (Touchstone) files and other vendor files. They are fetched from the official URLs in the manifest and verified against their sha256.
- When a manufacturer licence allows redistribution, the file may be committed, with its licence recorded in `REUSE.toml`.
